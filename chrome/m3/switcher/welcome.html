Firefox, in your colors

FMD3 restyles tabs, the address bar, menus and Firefox's own pages with Material You. Four short steps get it set up.

Light, dark, or both

Pick a side of the circle to keep Firefox light or dark. Match desktop switches with your system, day and night.

Pick a color

Firefox repaints as soon as you choose. Keep your desktop's colors, or pick one of these.

Change it any time

The theme page has every option: wallpaper colors, styles, contrast and layout. Open it with the palette button in the toolbar, Tools > Custom themes…, or Ctrl+Alt+M.

Open the theme page

You're set

FMD3 checks for updates once a day. Restart Firefox after an update to load it.

To remove FMD3 and restore your old setup, run ./install.sh --uninstall from the FMD3 folder. Details are in the README.
